# Icons in Windows dark mode (bug 31)

1. With Windows in dark mode (Settings > Personalization > Colors > "Choose your mode": Dark), open the main window: the buttons are dark with light icons; Upload shows an upload arrow (not a green "i") and Options shows three lines (not a wrench), the same pictures as in light mode.
2. Open the Upload button's menu and the tray menu: their icons are light and readable.
3. Switch Windows to light mode and restart Lightscreen: light buttons with dark icons, as in 2.5.
