# 2026-10-16: Stage 6, M08 done (X11 without QtX11Extras)

- New `os::x11Display()` (`QX11Info::display()` on Qt 5, `QNativeInterface::QX11Application::display()` on Qt 6) and `os::x11RootWindow()` (`DefaultRootWindow`, which is what `QX11Info::appRootWindow()` returned). The thirteen `QX11Info` call sites in `tools/os.cpp`, `tools/screenshot.cpp` and `tools/windowpicker.cpp` use them; none of those files includes `<QX11Info>` on Qt 6.
- UGlobalHotkeys (vendored) gets its xcb connection from `QX11Info::connection()` on Qt 5 and `QX11Application::connection()` on Qt 6 instead of `QPlatformNativeInterface`, so `gui-private` is gone and no private Qt headers are needed. `nativeEventFilter` takes a `UNativeEventResult *` (`long` on Qt 5, `qintptr` on Qt 6). Recorded in `tools/UGlobalHotkey/VENDORED.md`; why the vendored code does not call the `os::` helpers: `docs/decisions/2026-10-16-m08-x11-access-and-hotkey-smoke.md`.
